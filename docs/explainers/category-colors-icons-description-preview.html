Category final preview

The proposed 13 categories. Each icon sits on the daisyUI background token named beside it, with the paired content colour on the glyph. Toggle above to check both themes - a token that reads well in light does not necessarily read at all in dark.

Groceries (success)

Food, beverages, and household essentials bought to consume at home.

Dining Out (secondary)

Restaurants, coffee shops, takeout, delivery, and fast food.

Transportation (info)

Gas, public transit, rideshares, parking, and vehicle maintenance.

Utilities (accent)

Essential home services like electricity, water, internet, and phone plans.

Healthcare (error)

Doctor visits, pharmacy, dental, therapy, and medical bills.

Entertainment (primary)

Movies, concerts, gaming, hobbies, and streaming subscriptions.

Education (primary-content)

Tuition, courses, books, and school supplies.

Travel (secondary-content)

Flights, hotels, rental cars, and vacation expenses.

Personal Care (accent-content)

Haircuts, gym memberships, cosmetics, and hygiene products.

Gifts (success-content)

Presents for birthdays and holidays, as well as charity and donations.

Family & pets (info-content)

Childcare, baby supplies, pet care, vet bills, and family activities.

Loans & Debt (warning)

Mortgage payments, car loans, credit card payments, and student loans.

Uncategorized (warning-content)

Any transaction that doesn't have its own category.
