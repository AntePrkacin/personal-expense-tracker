Font pairing review, PET-79

The brief: a display face for page titles and the wordmark, less dense than IM Fell English SC, plus a body face. The lockup is drawn in the mapping you chose, a primary tile with a warning dollar sign.

Two hard constraints came out of measuring the fonts themselves. A display face wants more than one weight, because 25 of the 26 font-display call sites pair it with font-bold or font-semibold. And a body face wants a tnum feature, because six call sites rely on tabular-nums and it is inert without one.

1. The lockup

Every candidate is set to the font-size that matches your artwork's cap height exactly, computed from that family's own metrics. Density and letterform are therefore the only things differing.
