The monospace line under each label is the stored key, the value that travels in the API enum and lands in categories.icon.

The set is settled

Judged throughout on shape alone, at the two sizes shown: the large mark, and the 18px the app actually draws beside it, which is where a collision bites and where the old coins failed outright. 17 swaps, no icon added or removed, the 13 seeded untouched.

Residuals, knowingly accepted

The finished set was read whole one last time. These are what is left - all mild, all kept on purpose. The record exists so the next person does not report them as defects.

  • panda · baby

    Both a round face. panda adds ears and eye patches, baby a tuft. It arrived with the panda pick, which was itself the fix for squirrel against rabbit - the clearest example on this page of a swap moving a collision rather than removing it.

  • sailboat · tent

    Both resolve to a triangle at 18px; the sail sits over a hull line, the tent over a centre pole.

  • percent · pill

    A bold diagonal body, and pencil is a third member. percent keeps apart on its two rings, which is also what makes it the most legible mark in the money block.
