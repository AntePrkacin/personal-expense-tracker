How we keep the app safe

Our castle has more than one wall. Throttling is just the first guard.

🔒

The moat HTTPS

Everything between your phone and the app travels in a locked glass tube. Nobody on the road can peek at it.

🛡️

Guard 1: the counting bouncer throttling

Counts how often you knock and says "come back later" if you knock too much. See the two buckets below.

🎫

Guard 2: the one-time magic ticket login link

No passwords exist to steal. Your emailed link works exactly once, expires in 15 minutes, then turns to dust.

🤐

Guard 3: the silent doorman no peeking

Says the same thing to everyone, so a stranger cannot find out who is or is not a member.

🏠

Guard 4: everyone gets their own room a database each

Your things live in your own separate room. You cannot see into anyone else's, because their stuff is simply not in yours.

🪣 The bouncer keeps TWO buckets, not one

You are turned away if either bucket is empty. Two buckets catch two different tricks.

🎫
3
Letter bucket per 15 min

Counted per email address typed in. Stops flooding one inbox, no matter where the knocks come from.

🌐
15
Doorstep bucket per 15 min

Counted per visitor's network address. Stops one machine trying many different inboxes.

The bug we fixed on 2026-08-05: the bouncer was watching the big front gate instead of the people, so the whole crowd shared one doorstep bucket. Now everyone gets their own.

For a big event where lots of friends sign up together on the same WiFi, we can turn the doorstep bucket up for the day, then back down after.

🌱 One thing that is NOT a broken guard

Login emails sometimes land in spam. That is not a hole in the castle. Our castle is brand new, so the postman does not recognise our address yet. It gets better on its own as we send more real mail.
